Sponsors & collaborators

SPeC distinguishes monetary sponsors from non-monetary collaborators. Sponsors are formally recognized in the technical report and may co-author. Collaborators contribute feedback on rules, metrics, tracks, and experimental design. Industry collaborator logos are omitted from public-facing materials.

Sponsors

Anthropic, GXL, AWS, Biohub, and Highlanders. Sponsors may opt in to attend office hours to receive direct feedback from participants about their use of sponsor-provided tools.

Collaborators

Collaborators offer no monetary assistance and are strictly distinct from sponsors.

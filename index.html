Why SPeC?

Biomedical AI systems must perceive subtle clinical signals across imaging, pathology, waveforms, and structured records. SPeC brings together researchers, clinicians, and engineers to evaluate models on realistic, clinically motivated tasks with transparent metrics and reproducible protocols.

Our goal is to accelerate safe, generalizable perception models that can support discovery and decision-making in real healthcare settings.

  • Multimodal Imaging, text, signals & structured data
  • Open Public leaderboard & shared evaluation harness
  • Clinical Tasks grounded in real biomedical workflows

Challenge Tracks

SPeC is organized into complementary tracks designed to stress-test perception at different scales — from pixel-level findings to patient-level reasoning.

Medical Imaging Perception

Detect, segment, and classify findings across radiology and pathology modalities with emphasis on rare conditions and domain shift.

Multimodal Clinical Reasoning

Integrate images, reports, labs, and demographics to answer clinically relevant questions with calibrated uncertainty.

Temporal & Physiologic Signals

Interpret ECG, waveform, and time-series data for event detection, prognosis, and longitudinal monitoring tasks.

Robustness & Fairness

Evaluate performance across sites, devices, and patient subgroups to surface gaps before deployment.
